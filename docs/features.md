# IB Smart Campus — Features

A guide to what you can do on IB Smart Campus.

## Account & Profile
- Sign up and log in with email OTP, or set a password for quicker logins
- Set up your profile: username, bio, campus, and profile photo
- Make your account public or private
- Change your email or password anytime
- Earn points and build a daily streak
- Invite others with your personal referral code

## Communities
- Join communities for your college, company, or interest group
- Create your own community (goes through a quick approval step)
- Communities can have sub-communities under them (e.g. a college with department-level groups)
- Community privacy levels: public, restricted, or private
- Become a community moderator or admin to help manage it, including custom icon and banner images

## Institutes
- Join or follow an institute (e.g. your college/university as a whole)
- Institutes can have their own admins/moderators and host their own communities and events

## Posts & Feed
- Share posts with text, images, and flair tags in any community you're part of
- Upvote or downvote posts, and leave comments
- Create simple polls
- Track your own karma, post count, followers, and streak on your dashboard

## Events
- Discover events happening on campus: register first (answering any organizer questions), then RSVP
- Get put on a waitlist automatically if an event is full
- Fill out custom registration forms set by event organizers
- Get a QR check-in code for events you're going to
- Share any event with its full details (date, venue, description, link) to WhatsApp, X, Facebook, LinkedIn, Telegram, Instagram, email, or any app on your phone
- See every event you registered for, and its status, in your profile
- Past events are marked Completed and drop out of "Happening soon"
- Organize your own events: set date/time, venue, capacity, cover image, and a custom RSVP form
- Add co-admins to help manage your event
- Check people in at the door (including walk-ins who didn't RSVP online) and export the attendee list

## Certificates
- Receive event certificates by email automatically
- Verify any certificate's authenticity using its unique code

## Social & Messaging
- Search for and follow other users
- Send and accept follow requests (for private accounts)
- Send a chat request to start a conversation, and message 1:1 once accepted
- Get notified about events, community activity, posts, messages, and follow requests

## Moderation
- Reports and content go through moderator review for communities, events, and posts
- App-level moderators and admins keep the platform safe and organized
